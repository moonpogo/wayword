## 1) Field Clusters

### Quiet Software
- Phrase IDs: `P009 P010 P040 P041 P083 P084 P089 P094 P129 P130 P156`
- Terrain: low-theater software that stays present but non-coercive, with restraint as trust signal.
- Visual/UI pairing: low-contrast UI crops, thin typography quote cards, sparse observatory states, empty-space-led compositions.
- Overuse risks: can become vague softness if not anchored by concrete terms like `return`, `signal`, `trace`.

### Observation vs Evaluation
- Phrase IDs: `P004 P005 P014 P015 P124 P131 P141 P148 P165`
- Terrain: anti-diagnostic stance, pattern visibility without judgment claims.
- Visual/UI pairing: reflection card crops, side-by-side "signal shown / interpretation withheld" diagrams.
- Overuse risks: repetitive moral framing if every use repeats "does not diagnose" explicitly.

### Seasons and Return
- Phrase IDs: `P018 P028 P029 P035 P041 P127 P129 P134 P145 P156 P164`
- Terrain: time as climate and recurrence, not progress ladder.
- Visual/UI pairing: season-wheel crops, radial diagrams, archival timestamp stacks.
- Overuse risks: poetic seasonal language can drift into decorative weather metaphors without behavioral grounding.

### Writing as Instrument
- Phrase IDs: `P001 P006 P021 P022 P049 P121 P158`
- Terrain: writing surface as perceptual apparatus, not optimization engine.
- Visual/UI pairing: editor close crops, cursor states, quiet toolmark iconography.
- Overuse risks: "instrument" can feel abstract if not paired with concrete user loop moments.

### Blank Page / Initiation Friction
- Phrase IDs: `P023 P053 P054 P058 P059 P136 P162`
- Terrain: entry pressure, thin signal, continuing without theatrical self-judgment.
- Visual/UI pairing: empty editor states, first-line prompt crops, low-signal reflection card.
- Overuse risks: too much low-signal vocabulary can read as refusal rather than invitation.

### Reflection Surfaces
- Phrase IDs: `P052 P055 P056 P057 P060 P061 P062 P068 P070 P071 P072 P073`

- Visual/UI pairing: mirror card stacks, mono-weight quote cards, "before/after line movement" micro diagrams.

### Observatory Language

## 6) Drift Detection

### Startup-language drift
- At risk: `P075` (if framed like activation milestone), `P151` (if used as brand governance slogan), `P139` (if treated as growth progression marker).
- Correction: pair with observational anchors (`P004 P014 P129`) and avoid conversion framing.

### Mysticism drift
- At risk: `P120 P039 P047 P048`.
- Correction: tether to evidence phrases (`P065 P137 P163`) and concrete artifact visuals.

### AI-slop cadence drift
- At risk: symmetrical doctrine pairings repeated too often (`P017 P018 P019 P020` in serial).
- Correction: rotate with rough fragments (`P055 P057 P068 P071`) and incomplete statements.

### Productivity-culture drift
- At risk: `P079` and challenge language if reframed as performance loop.
- Correction: keep practice language attached to recurrence and return, not outcomes.

### Too polished / symmetrical
- At risk: any stacked "X over Y" chains and polished triads.
- Correction: introduce clipped operational lines (`P053 P060 P061`) and unresolved atmospheric fragments (`P135 P136`).

### Too abstract to visualize
- At risk: `P036 behavioral climatologies`, `P092 contradiction plurality doctrine`.
- Correction: reserve for essays/docs, not social cards. Pair with diagrams if public.

## 7) Rhythm and Cadence Doctrine

- Sentence lengths: strongest Wayword lines cluster in 3-8 words (`P002 P014 P017 P019`) or 2-clause clipped lines (`P053 P060 P071`).
- Fragment usage: high-value fragments avoid full argument closure (`P129 P132 P156`). Keep partiality.
- Asymmetry patterns: line pairs often carry uneven clause weight, not slogan symmetry. Example pattern: short assertion plus procedural tail (`P053`).
- Accumulation behavior: strongest stacks move from concrete surface to philosophical frame, not reverse.
- Observational phrasing: verbs are visible-pattern verbs, not identity verbs. Keep "returns/surfaces/shifts" class.
- Scientific-literary balance: metric humility plus sensory language. "signal", "pattern", "surface" with "silence", "season", "return".
- Ambiguity without vagueness: preserve uncertainty by limiting claim scope, not by adding ornamental words.
- Authorial roughness rule: allow clipped severity, occasional unresolved fragment, and non-uniform sentence rhythm.
