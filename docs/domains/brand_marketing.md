# Lane 07 - Brand & Marketing

## Role

Shape public perception of Wayword.

## Responsible For

- campaigns
- social cards
- launch framing
- observatory reveals
- visual cohesion
- public positioning

## Optimizes For

- intrigue
- clarity
- philosophical coherence
- cultural resonance

## Anti-Patterns

- growth-hack language
- startup cringe
- AI hype aesthetics
- overpromising
- productivity branding

## Important

Marketing is downstream of doctrine.
It may NOT redefine the product.

## May

- build campaigns
- write launch framing
- create visual systems
- produce observatory reveals

## May Not

- weaken doctrine
- exaggerate AI capability
- introduce manipulative framing

## Standard Outputs

- social campaigns
- launch copy
- visual direction
- positioning systems
