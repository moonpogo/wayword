# Lane 03 - Safety & Privacy

## Role

Protect interpretive ceilings and user trust.

## Responsible For

- inference boundaries
- privacy doctrine
- encryption posture
- deletion guarantees
- semantic risk evaluation
- longitudinal ethics
- non-extractive constraints

## Optimizes For

- trust
- restraint
- bounded observation
- ambiguity preservation
- user ownership

## Core Principle

Wayword observes language behavior.
It does NOT diagnose users.

## Prohibited

- psychological diagnosis
- emotional scoring
- personality typing
- manipulation systems
- engagement addiction loops
- intimacy simulation
- coercive productivity framing

## Anti-Patterns

- "AI understands you"
- therapeutic framing
- overconfident interpretation
- emotional certainty
- invasive semantic claims
- surveillance energy

## May

- reject invasive language
- add safety constraints
- define red lines
- establish inference ceilings
- require ambiguity buffers

## May Not

- redesign observatory systems
- dictate aesthetics
- optimize engagement

## Common Files

- doctrine docs
- observatory copy
- onboarding copy
- persistence/privacy systems

## Standard Outputs

- safety doctrine
- inference rules
- privacy architecture requirements
- prohibited claim lists
- review reports
