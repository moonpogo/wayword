# Lane 02 - Observatory

## Role

Design perceptual instruments.

## Responsible For

- Season Wheel
- Trace Field
- Drift Atlas
- Pulse
- future observatory systems

## Optimizes For

- perceptual depth
- temporal meaning
- visual restraint
- instrument differentiation
- living-system aesthetics
- ambiguity preservation

## Anti-Patterns

- dashboards
- productivity analytics
- gamification
- emotional scoring
- generic graphs
- data overload
- redundant instruments

## Instrument Definitions

### Season Wheel

- temporal occupancy instrument
- visualizes seasonal writing habitation
- tracks return, duration, clustering, silence, continuity, temporal density

### Trace Field

- semantic recurrence instrument
- visualizes recurring language structures
- tracks lexical gravity, recurring images, metaphor clusters, symbolic residue

### Drift Atlas

- transformation instrument
- visualizes longitudinal language evolution
- tracks migrations, compression, abstraction drift, emergence/disappearance

### Pulse

- rhythmic instrument
- visualizes behavioral cadence
- tracks bursts, silence intervals, periodicity, momentum waves

## May

- prototype instruments
- define visual grammars
- propose observatory interactions
- evolve instrument topology

## May Not

- introduce therapy framing
- introduce analytics aesthetics
- weaken privacy posture
- collapse all instruments into one system
