# Alpha Definition Packet

Initiative: Road to Real - Phase 0 to Phase 1 Operationalization  
Phase: 0 (Operationalization)  
Status: Canonical alpha retention + learning structure defined

## Mission

Define the bounded alpha structure required to:
- validate retention
- observe emotional attachment
- test longitudinal behavior
- refine onboarding and observatory coherence
- learn without scaling prematurely

This packet defines:
- who alpha is for
- how users enter the system
- what activation means
- what retention means
- how interviews operate
- how qualitative signals are captured
- what hypotheses Phase 1 is attempting to validate

This packet is not:
- growth strategy
- mass acquisition
- public launch planning
- marketing expansion
- virality optimization

## Primary Alpha Objective

Determine whether Wayword creates:
- meaningful return behavior
- emotional continuity
- longitudinal attachment
- observatory curiosity
- sustained writing recurrence

Alpha is not validating:
- mass-market appeal
- productivity utility
- AI novelty
- social growth mechanics

## Alpha Scale (Lock)

Initial target:
- 25-40 users

Maximum during initial alpha:
- 50 users

Rationale:
- small enough for direct observation, manual interviews, behavioral interpretation, onboarding refinement, and doctrine monitoring
- avoids premature scale distortion

## Target User Profiles

### Priority Users

Seek:
- reflective writers
- designers
- artists
- musicians
- researchers
- thoughtful engineers
- journalers
- readers
- people with sustained internal practices

Users should:
- tolerate ambiguity
- appreciate restraint
- value continuity
- engage voluntarily without gamification pressure

### Deprioritized Users

Do not optimize for:
- productivity maximizers
- AI-output seekers
- growth hackers
- dopamine-loop users
- engagement optimizers
- lifehack culture

Rationale:
- these profiles distort product interpretation and retention signal quality

## Onboarding Structure

### Goal

Reduce intimidation while establishing:
- observational framing
- emotional safety
- continuity expectation
- observatory anticipation

Without:
- over-explaining
- mystical framing
- productivity framing
- therapeutic framing

### Onboarding Flow

1. Entry
- immediate access to writing surface
- minimal friction
- no feature overwhelm

2. Calibration Runs
- short low-pressure prompts
- no observatory overload
- gradual continuity formation

3. Early Reflection
- restrained Mirror responses
- avoid over-interpretation
- encourage return rather than analysis

4. Observatory Emergence
- observatories appear progressively
- framed as accumulated traces
- not achievement unlocks

## Activation Definition (Canonical)

Activation occurs when user:
- completes multiple writing sessions
- and voluntarily returns
- and engages with observatory surface
- and demonstrates continuity awareness

Activation is not:
- first login
- first write
- first save

Activation is recognition of longitudinal continuity.

## Retention Definition (Canonical)

Retention means:
- recurring voluntary writing behavior over time
- repeated observatory interaction
- sustained emotional relevance
- continued saving and return

Not:
- raw app opens
- passive browsing
- forced engagement

## Meaningful Session (Lock)

A meaningful session includes:
- completed writing pass
- and save event or observatory interaction
- and evidence of voluntary continuation behavior

Examples:
- user returns to revisit observatory
- user performs additional writing voluntarily
- user saves multiple runs over time

## Retention Targets

By end of alpha:
- 25 active users
- 40%+ weekly retention
- 3+ sessions/week among retained users
- repeated observatory return behavior
- at least 5 strong emotional attachment signals

## Strong Attachment Signals

Examples:
- "I missed using this"
- "I keep thinking about this"
- "I noticed something about myself"
- "This feels strangely personal"
- "I want to see what emerges over time"

These are core product indicators.

## Anti-Delusion Metrics

Track:
- percent of users who correctly understand Wayword purpose
- percent of users revisiting observatories voluntarily
- percent of users repeatedly saving runs
- percent of users misunderstanding product framing

Track confusion with:
- productivity apps
- therapy systems
- AI assistant framing
- journaling tracker assumptions

Rationale:
- aesthetic fascination is not product validation

## Interview Cadence

### Foundational Rule

Interviews exist to:
- observe reality
- refine understanding
- identify confusion
- detect emotional resonance

Not:
- seek praise
- validate founder identity
- harvest compliments

### Suggested Cadence

- onboarding interview/check-in
- week 1 follow-up
- week 3 retention interview
- post-observatory-emergence interview

### Questions To Explore

- What made you return?
- What felt unclear?
- What observatory stayed in memory?
- Did any surface feel judgmental?
- Did the system feel calming or pressuring?
- Did you feel observed or categorized?
- What felt meaningful?
- What felt excessive?

## Alpha Behavioral Hypotheses

Hypothesis 1:
- longitudinal continuity creates stronger attachment than isolated writing feedback

Hypothesis 2:
- restrained observatories increase curiosity and return behavior

Hypothesis 3:
- sparse observatory states can still feel emotionally meaningful

Hypothesis 4:
- users tolerate ambiguity when emotional coherence remains strong

Hypothesis 5:
- trust posture and restraint increase perceived intimacy

## Doctrine Risks

Avoid:
- onboarding overload
- over-explanation
- therapy-adjacent framing
- optimization framing
- productivity language
- engagement manipulation
- excessive notification pressure

## Failure Conditions

FAIL if:
- users consistently misunderstand the product
- observatories create pressure or anxiety
- users feel psychologically categorized
- return behavior remains weak
- onboarding overwhelms users
- emotional resonance fails to persist after novelty fades

## Phase 0 Success Condition (Alpha Packet Context)

Success if:
- target users are explicitly defined
- onboarding structure is bounded
- activation and retention definitions are canonical
- interview cadence is operationalized
- alpha hypotheses are explicit
- implementation ambiguity materially reduced

No expansion into growth loops, social systems, referral systems, or virality mechanics is authorized during alpha validation.
