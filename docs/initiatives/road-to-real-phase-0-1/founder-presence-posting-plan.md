# Founder Presence Posting Plan

Date: 2026-05-26

## Voice Commitments

- observational over declarative
- specific over sloganized
- restrained over launch-hype
- partial over over-resolved

## Weekly Mix

- 3 field notes
- 2 visual artifacts
- 1 recruitment invitation
- 1 deeper Substack post or excerpt

## Post Structure Defaults

- Line 1: concrete observation
- Line 2: implication for product/feeling
- Optional line 3: unresolved question

## Cadence for Alpha Window (First 4 Weeks)

- Week 1: explain thesis + recruit first cohort
- Week 2: post first friction learnings + observatory fragments
- Week 3: emphasize return stories and sparse-state meaning
- Week 4: publish early retention reflections (without overclaiming)

## Red Flags To Avoid

- polished startup cadence
- pseudo-philosophical vagueness
- advice-guru framing
- "we fixed everything" tone

## Operating Rule

If no specific artifact or observation is available, post less.
