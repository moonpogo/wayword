# Emotional Tone Audit

Date: 2026-05-26

## Tone Target

Observational, restrained, curious, emotionally safe.

## Tone Successes

- Most first-run and observatory language avoids evaluative or diagnostic posture.
- Sparse-state lines preserve humility and avoid fake optimism.
- Product voice remains non-coercive and non-gamified.

## Tone Risks

- Overuse of "pattern" language can imply certainty too early.
- Some users may interpret restraint as absence of care if founder framing is missing.

## Anti-Tone Drift Checks

- No productivity-maxxing language found in reviewed alpha surfaces.
- No streak/notification/engagement-adjacent framing introduced.
- No AI-assistant voice added to onboarding or reflection surfaces.

## Audit Verdict

Tone is alpha-appropriate and doctrine-aligned. Highest risk is not language harshness; it is users bringing incompatible expectations.
