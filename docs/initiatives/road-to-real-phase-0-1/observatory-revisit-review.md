# Observatory Revisit Review

Date: 2026-05-26

## Review Focus

Assess whether observatory states create return tension without analytics/dashboard behavior.

## What Is Working

- Current Season acts as a continuity anchor rather than a metric card.
- Pattern formation language signals emergence pacing appropriately.
- Sparse states are restrained and less punitive than prior phrasing.

## Where Revisit Can Fail

- Users expecting immediate cross-run certainty may disengage after first sparse revisit.
- "No stable pattern" states need recruitment context to prevent disappointment framing.

## Emotional Cadence Assessment

- Opening mood: quiet, observational
- Mid mood: ambiguous but coherent
- Return invitation: present, not forceful
- Dashboard drift risk: low in current copy and structure

## Revisit Retention Hypothesis

Users who complete 3+ saved runs and open observatory twice are materially more likely to return voluntarily within 7 days than users who only consume post-run reflection.

## Suggested Alpha Interview Prompt

"When you reopened Patterns, did it feel like a place accumulating your writing or a static report?"
