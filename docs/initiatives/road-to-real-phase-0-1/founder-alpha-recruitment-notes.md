# Founder Alpha Recruitment Notes

Date: 2026-05-26

## Positioning Line

Wayword is a reflective writing instrument that helps people notice recognizable traces in their own language over time.

## Recruitment Talking Points

- This is not productivity software.
- It is not scoring your writing.
- Value appears through recurrence and accumulation across runs.
- Early sessions may feel subtle by design.

## Best-Fit Early Users

- People already journaling or writing informally.
- People curious about voice, pattern, and emotional cadence.
- People open to returning 3-5 times before judging value.

## Suggested Channel Fragments

- BlueSky/Substack: "quiet evidence of return" framing with observatory screenshots.
- Pinterest/visual artifacts: Current Season + sparse-to-emergent sequence fragments.
- Founder devlog: short notes on what changed after repeated runs.

## Founder Interview Questions

- "What made you come back for another run?"
- "When did the observatory begin to feel like yours?"
- "Did anything feel evaluative instead of reflective?"
- "What would make you return this week without reminders?"
