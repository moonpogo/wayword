Subject: Opening a small alpha for Wayword

I’m opening a small alpha cohort for Wayword.

Wayword is a reflective writing instrument. You do short runs, submit, and over time the system accumulates recognizable traces in your language.

It is not a writing coach. It is not a productivity tracker. It is built around observation, recurrence, and return.

I’m inviting 20-40 people who are willing to complete five runs across one week and share candid feedback about emotional coherence, not feature requests.

If you want in, reply here.
