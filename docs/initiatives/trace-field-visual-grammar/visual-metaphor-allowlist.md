# Visual Metaphor Allowlist

Initiative: Trace Field Visual Grammar  
Continuation: Visual Metaphor Allowlist/Blocklist Decision Packet  
Scope: Docs-only doctrine and decision language

## Purpose

Define visual metaphor families compatible with Wayword doctrine and Trace Field evidence posture.

Compatibility requires:
- incompleteness preserved
- inspectability preserved
- ambiguity preserved
- reversibility back to evidence
- non-diagnostic posture

## 1) Restrained Field Distributions

Description:
- dispersed, low-drama point/region fields showing recurrence presence without singular authority centers

Why epistemically safer:
- avoids single “truth focal point” implication

Evidence relationship:
- each region must map to traceable recurrence evidence and threshold context

Ambiguity handling:
- allows multiple weak fields to coexist

Inspectability implications:
- field segments must be explorable back to source traces

Overclaim risk level:
- low to moderate

Doctrine notes:
- aligns with observational posture over diagnosis

QA watchpoints:
- reject if density styling implies certainty rather than frequency context

## 2) Discrete Trace Marks

Description:
- individual, legible marks representing bounded recurrence events or motifs

Why epistemically safer:
- preserves separability of evidence units

Evidence relationship:
- one mark class must map to one or more explicit recurrence primitives

Ambiguity handling:
- marks may remain unresolved without forced grouping

Inspectability implications:
- strong trace-to-source reversibility

Overclaim risk level:
- low

Doctrine notes:
- supports evidence-first rendering discipline

QA watchpoints:
- reject mark smoothing that obscures provenance

## 3) Sparse Cartographic Structures

Description:
- map-like sparse arrangement emphasizing relation and interval over heat/intensity

Why epistemically safer:
- discourages score-like interpretation

Evidence relationship:
- cartographic placements must correspond to defined recurrence relation logic

Ambiguity handling:
- gaps remain explicit and meaningful

Inspectability implications:
- map nodes/paths must open into evidence traces

Overclaim risk level:
- moderate

Doctrine notes:
- compatible if precision theater is avoided

QA watchpoints:
- reject pseudo-geographic certainty cues unsupported by evidence

## 4) Observational Lattices

Description:
- lightweight lattice/grid scaffolds that hold observations without implying ranking

Why epistemically safer:
- structure supports readability without emotional hierarchy

Evidence relationship:
- lattice cells/slots map to explicit recurrence dimensions only

Ambiguity handling:
- unresolved cells remain unresolved, not auto-filled

Inspectability implications:
- each occupied position must link to evidence source path

Overclaim risk level:
- moderate

Doctrine notes:
- acceptable if not converted into dashboard KPI framing

QA watchpoints:
- reject if lattice becomes performance/progression scoreboard

## 5) Evidence-Linked Clusters With Visible Uncertainty

Description:
- cluster forms allowed only with explicit uncertainty indicators and evidence links

Why epistemically safer:
- prevents clusters from masquerading as identity labels

Evidence relationship:
- cluster membership rules and support thresholds must be inspectable

Ambiguity handling:
- ambiguous memberships stay partial/soft, not definitive

Inspectability implications:
- user can inspect cluster support and contradictions

Overclaim risk level:
- moderate to high

Doctrine notes:
- permitted only under strong anti-identity safeguards

QA watchpoints:
- reject if clusters are interpreted as personality or emotional state types

## 6) Interruption/Gap-Aware Structures

Description:
- visual grammars that explicitly encode breaks, absences, and discontinuities

Why epistemically safer:
- avoids false continuity and narrative inevitability

Evidence relationship:
- interruptions must reflect actual missing/weak recurrence windows

Ambiguity handling:
- discontinuity preserved as observational fact

Inspectability implications:
- interruption states must be traceable to evidence absence or low support

Overclaim risk level:
- low

Doctrine notes:
- strongly aligns with silence/absence as meaningful

QA watchpoints:
- reject if gaps are visually penalized as deficits

## 7) Partial/Incomplete Formations

Description:
- intentionally incomplete forms showing evidence limits and unresolved signal

Why epistemically safer:
- communicates bounded knowledge

Evidence relationship:
- incompleteness tied to explicit threshold/coverage limits

Ambiguity handling:
- protects unresolved meaning space

Inspectability implications:
- users can inspect why form is partial

Overclaim risk level:
- low

Doctrine notes:
- directly supports anti-omniscience posture

QA watchpoints:
- reject if partial state is smoothed into complete narrative shape

## 8) Quiet-State Rendering

Description:
- low-intensity visual state when recurrence is sparse or uncertain

Why epistemically safer:
- prevents decorative filling and fake activity

Evidence relationship:
- quiet state tied to low-support evidence conditions

Ambiguity handling:
- keeps uncertainty and silence visible

Inspectability implications:
- quiet regions should still allow evidence checks where present

Overclaim risk level:
- low

Doctrine notes:
- consistent with restrained observational language

QA watchpoints:
- reject if quiet state is framed as failure or pathology

## 9) Trace-to-Source Pathways

Description:
- explicit pathways from macro visual forms to source traces and excerpts

Why epistemically safer:
- enforces evidence accountability

Evidence relationship:
- direct reversible mapping from render feature to evidence origin

Ambiguity handling:
- supports revisitation and reinterpretation

Inspectability implications:
- strongest inspectability requirement in allowlist

Overclaim risk level:
- low

Doctrine notes:
- mandatory for evidentiary observatory posture

QA watchpoints:
- reject any metaphor family that cannot support reversible evidence navigation

## Allowlist Guardrail

Allowed families are conditional, not unconditional.
If a family cannot preserve inspectability, ambiguity, and evidence reversibility in a given rendering proposal, it is rejected for that proposal.
