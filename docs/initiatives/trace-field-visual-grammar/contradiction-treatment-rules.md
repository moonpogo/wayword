# Contradiction Treatment Rules

Initiative: Trace Field Visual Grammar  
Continuation: Visual Contradiction & Weak-Signal Treatment Decision Packet  
Scope: Docs-only visual doctrine

## 1) Why Contradiction Is Expected

Contradictory recurrence is normal in longitudinal writing.
Language patterns can diverge, reverse, or coexist without requiring unified explanation.

## 2) Why Contradiction Is Not Failure

Contradiction is an observational condition, not a system error.
It indicates plural evidence states, not broken inference.

## 3) Rules For Coexisting Contradictory Signals

- contradictory signals may co-appear without hierarchical dominance
- coexisting contradictions should remain legible as separate evidentiary threads
- rendering must not imply one signal is “truer” unless evidence thresholds explicitly justify that

## 4) Rules Against Winner-Take-All Synthesis

- no winner-take-all visual merge for contradictory signals
- contradiction resolution by style preference is prohibited
- contradictions remain multiple when evidence remains multiple

## 5) Rules Against Dominant Narrative Collapse

- do not collapse contradictory recurrence into one clean storyline
- avoid narrative framing that converts contradiction into inevitability
- contradiction should resist singular “arc completion” aesthetics

## 6) Rules For Contradictory Cluster Coexistence

- clusters with conflicting recurrence relations may coexist
- conflicting cluster memberships should remain explicit where support overlaps
- no forced reassignment to preserve visual neatness

## 7) Rules For Contradictory Temporal Recurrence

- temporal contradictions (return + interruption, expansion + compression) should remain visible
- time rendering must not smooth away recurrence reversals
- discontinuities should preserve observed contradiction, not hide it

## 8) Rules For Contradiction Inspectability

- each contradictory form must map to its own evidence pathway
- users must be able to inspect supporting traces for each side of contradiction
- inspectability must expose why contradiction persists

## 9) Rules Against Causal-Story Implication

- contradiction rendering must not imply causal psychological storylines
- no visual language that suggests internal conflict diagnosis
- no destiny arc semantics that reinterpret contradiction as life-trajectory certainty

## 10) QA Contradiction Checks

PASS only if:
- contradictions remain plural when evidence is plural
- no winner-take-all collapse occurs without explicit threshold basis
- contradictory temporal signals remain visible
- contradiction pathways are inspectable to source traces
- no identity/diagnosis implication emerges

Automatic FAIL if:
- contradiction is force-merged for visual coherence
- contradiction is rendered as psychological conflict diagnosis
- contradiction is converted into singular narrative destiny arc
- inspectability degrades under contradiction styling

## 11) Founder Decision Targets

- approve plural-contradiction doctrine as binding
- approve anti-winner-take-all rendering rule
- approve contradiction QA fail triggers and non-causal constraints

## Contradiction Guardrail

Contradiction must remain:
- observable
- inspectable
- plural
- unresolved when evidence is unresolved

Contradiction must not become:
- noise cleanup target
- forced synthesis pressure
- psychological conflict diagnosis
- narrative destiny arc
