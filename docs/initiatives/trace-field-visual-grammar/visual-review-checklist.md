# Visual Review Checklist

Initiative: Trace Field Visual Grammar  
Continuation: Visual QA Gate Matrix Packet  
Scope: Operational checklist for evaluating future Trace Field visual proposals

## Use Instructions

- Apply this checklist before any founder review packet.
- If any automatic rejection condition is met, stop review and mark NO-GO.
- Record outcomes with concrete evidence references, not impression-only notes.

## A) Observational Posture

Reviewer questions:
- Does the proposal describe recurrence behavior rather than user identity?
- Is recurrence presented without diagnosis or personality inference?

Red-flag heuristics:
- visuals feel like personal truth maps
- claims read as hidden user-state detection

Automatic rejection conditions:
- any diagnosis, identity labeling, or behavioral scoring implication

Escalation conditions:
- uncertainty whether framing crosses inference ceiling

Conductor pause conditions:
- repeated proposals drifting toward identity interpretation

Founder escalation criteria:
- unresolved disagreement on observational vs interpretive boundary

## B) Inspectability Over Beauty

Reviewer questions:
- Can each salient visual element be traced to source evidence?
- Is traceability clearer than decorative styling?

Red-flag heuristics:
- visually striking core forms with unclear evidence lineage

Automatic rejection conditions:
- high-salience elements lack inspectable evidence mapping

Escalation conditions:
- trace path exists only for subset of important marks

Conductor pause conditions:
- aesthetic novelty repeatedly prioritized over inspectability

Founder escalation criteria:
- tradeoff request to reduce inspectability for visual smoothness

## C) Ambiguity Honesty

Reviewer questions:
- Are unresolved/partial patterns shown as unresolved/partial?
- Does the visual avoid forced synthesis into one conclusion?

Red-flag heuristics:
- contradiction disappears in final composition

Automatic rejection conditions:
- ambiguity erased or reframed as certainty

Escalation conditions:
- review cannot determine whether ambiguity was legitimately reduced

Conductor pause conditions:
- repeated synthesis pressure with no evidence basis

Founder escalation criteria:
- policy disagreement on acceptable ambiguity visibility

## D) Contradiction Plurality

Reviewer questions:
- Do contradictory signals remain co-present when evidence is contradictory?
- Is there any winner-take-all collapse?

Red-flag heuristics:
- single dominant narrative absorbs conflicting clusters

Automatic rejection conditions:
- contradiction collapsed for coherence without explicit evidence basis

Escalation conditions:
- contradictions handled inconsistently across similar cases

Conductor pause conditions:
- ongoing narrative-collapse tendency across proposals

Founder escalation criteria:
- unresolved conflict on contradiction treatment policy

## E) Silence/Absence Non-Deficit Treatment

Reviewer questions:
- Are silence and gaps represented as valid observational states?
- Is absence free from deficiency signaling?

Red-flag heuristics:
- empty states imply failure, alarm, or broken behavior

Automatic rejection conditions:
- silence coded as pathology, deficit, or productivity failure

Escalation conditions:
- unclear whether inactivity styling implies negative judgment

Conductor pause conditions:
- repeated emotionalized silence framing

Founder escalation criteria:
- unresolved disagreement on quiet-state semantics

## F) Weak-Signal Boundedness

Reviewer questions:
- Are weak signals visibly low-support?
- Is low-support recurrence prevented from dramatic emphasis?

Red-flag heuristics:
- weak patterns presented as hidden deep truth

Automatic rejection conditions:
- weak signals masquerade as strong recurrence
- weak signals removed without explainable rationale

Escalation conditions:
- support-level definitions unclear or inconsistently applied

Conductor pause conditions:
- low-support recurrence repeatedly used as atmosphere

Founder escalation criteria:
- unresolved policy question on weak-signal visibility vs suppression

## G) Threshold Operational Humility

Reviewer questions:
- Are thresholds framed as operational settings, not objective truth?
- Are near-threshold cases treated provisionally?

Red-flag heuristics:
- threshold crossing presented as natural category boundary

Automatic rejection conditions:
- threshold logic implies oracle authority or hidden certainty

Escalation conditions:
- threshold changes lack rationale/versioning clarity

Conductor pause conditions:
- recurring hidden-authority drift in threshold framing

Founder escalation criteria:
- unresolved decision on threshold governance strictness

## H) Suppression Auditability

Reviewer questions:
- Is suppression rationale explicit and reviewable?
- Can reviewers inspect what was suppressed and why?

Red-flag heuristics:
- coherence improved by unexplained disappearance of messy evidence

Automatic rejection conditions:
- opaque suppression or inconsistent suppression across equivalent cases

Escalation conditions:
- suppression bias concerns cannot be resolved in review

Conductor pause conditions:
- suppression repeatedly used as hidden curation

Founder escalation criteria:
- unresolved conflict on acceptable suppression transparency level

## I) Metaphor Doctrine Safety

Reviewer questions:
- Does the proposal stay inside allowed metaphor families?
- Are blocked metaphors absent in both visuals and language?

Red-flag heuristics:
- aura, personality map, pseudo-medical instrumentation, cosmic destiny cues

Automatic rejection conditions:
- any blocked metaphor family appears

Escalation conditions:
- uncertain classification of a metaphor family

Conductor pause conditions:
- metaphor exploration drifting into decorative taxonomy

Founder escalation criteria:
- unresolved dispute on allowlist/blocklist boundaries

## J) First-Glance Implication Check

Reviewer questions:
- In first glance, does this feel observational and evidentiary?
- Or does it immediately imply diagnosis, identity truth, or scoring?

Red-flag heuristics:
- "it looks authoritative before evidence is visible"

Automatic rejection conditions:
- first-glance implication suggests diagnosis, identity mapping, or hidden truth engine

Escalation conditions:
- review team split on first-glance implication risk

Conductor pause conditions:
- repeated first-glance failures across iterations

Founder escalation criteria:
- unresolved disagreement on acceptable first-glance authority signals

## Checklist Outcome Rule

- APPROVE FOR FOUNDER PACKET: no automatic rejections; escalation items resolved or clearly routed.
- NO-GO: any automatic rejection, unresolved hidden-authority risk, or unresolved narrative-collapse risk.

## Minimum Evidence Bundle Required For Review

- proposal visual rationale note
- evidence-lineage map for salient forms
- threshold/suppression rationale table
- contradiction + ambiguity handling examples
- silence/absence handling examples
- weak-signal handling examples
- metaphor-family classification note
