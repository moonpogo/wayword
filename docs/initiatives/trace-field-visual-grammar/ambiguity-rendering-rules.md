# Ambiguity Rendering Rules

Initiative: Trace Field Visual Grammar  
Continuation: Visual Ambiguity & Silence Rendering Decision Packet  
Scope: Docs-only visual doctrine

## 1) Why Ambiguity Is First-Class, Not Failure

Ambiguity is expected in observational systems.
Trace Field observes recurrence patterns, not hidden identity truth.

Ambiguity indicates:
- bounded evidence
- plural possible readings
- unresolved pattern relationships

It does not indicate:
- system weakness
- missing intelligence
- diagnostic uncertainty requiring forced interpretation

## 2) Rules For Unresolved Recurrence

- unresolved recurrence must remain unresolved in visual form
- unresolved states must not be auto-resolved by smoothing, grouping, or narrative labels
- unresolvedness must remain inspectable with evidence links

## 3) Rules For Contradictory Recurrence

- contradictory signals may coexist without forced synthesis
- contradictions should be represented as co-present, not collapsed
- visual grammar must avoid “winner-take-all” resolution cues when evidence is mixed

## 4) Rules For Weak Recurrence

- weak recurrence should be visibly low-support, not hidden or exaggerated
- low-support cues must not mimic high-confidence styling
- weak recurrence may be suppressed when below agreed threshold rules

## 5) Rules For Partial Recurrence

- partial recurrence must render as partial
- partial evidence should not inherit full-form visual authority
- partial forms must keep reversible links to supporting traces

## 6) Rules For Uncertain Clustering

- uncertain clusters must be marked as uncertain
- cluster boundaries may remain soft when membership is unstable
- clusters must never be rendered as identity categories

## 7) Rules For Noisy States

- noisy states must not be stylized into false depth
- noise should appear as unresolved/noisy, not transformed into decorative complexity
- noisy states may remain quiet rather than theatrically represented

## 8) Rules Against Forced Synthesis

- do not force all recurrence into a single coherent visual narrative
- do not synthesize contradictory signals into one definitive shape
- do not prioritize elegance over evidentiary honesty

## 9) Rules Against Singular “True Interpretation” Rendering

- no single “master interpretation” form
- no visual language that implies essence extraction
- no focal glyph or emblem presented as definitive user truth

## 10) How Ambiguity Remains Inspectable

- ambiguity states must support trace-to-source reversibility
- users should be able to inspect why a state is unresolved/partial/noisy
- uncertainty and contradiction must expose evidence lineage, not conceal it

## 11) QA Ambiguity Checks

PASS only if:
- unresolved states remain unresolved
- contradictory states are not force-resolved
- weak/partial/noisy signals are clearly differentiated
- no identity or diagnosis implications emerge from ambiguity styling
- inspectability pathways remain intact

Automatic FAIL if:
- ambiguity is rendered as hidden-depth mysticism
- unresolvedness is aesthetic fog without evidence links
- contradiction is erased for visual neatness
- ambiguity collapses into identity inference

## 12) Founder Decision Targets

- approve ambiguity-first rule: unresolved evidence may remain unresolved
- approve anti-forced-synthesis rule as binding visual doctrine
- approve QA fail conditions for ambiguity collapse

## Ambiguity Guardrail

Ambiguity must remain:
- observable
- revisitable
- inspectable
- non-stigmatized

Ambiguity must not become:
- decorative fog
- mystical symbolism
- hidden-depth theater
- fake complexity
