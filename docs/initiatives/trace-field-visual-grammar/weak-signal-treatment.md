# Weak Signal Treatment

Initiative: Trace Field Visual Grammar  
Continuation: Visual Contradiction & Weak-Signal Treatment Decision Packet  
Scope: Docs-only visual doctrine

## 1) Why Weak Signals Matter

Weak signals can indicate emerging or intermittent recurrence.
They are part of observational continuity and should not be erased by default.

## 2) Why Weak Signals Are Dangerous

Weak signals are vulnerable to overclaim and underclaim:
- overclaim risk: styled as meaningful certainty they do not support
- underclaim risk: hidden entirely without rationale, creating opaque suppression bias

## 3) Rules For Low-Support Recurrence

- low-support recurrence must remain visibly low-support
- low-support forms cannot use high-confidence visual salience
- low-support states require explicit threshold context

## 4) Rules For Threshold Visibility

- threshold logic should be legible in doctrine and inspectability pathways
- users should be able to tell when a signal is near threshold vs clearly above threshold
- threshold boundaries must not be implied as natural truths; they are operational limits

## 5) Rules For Suppression vs Visibility

- suppression is allowed only with explicit rationale and consistent rules
- when suppressed, rationale class should remain inspectable (for internal QA/trace review)
- visibility should be preferred when weak signal is materially informative and non-misleading

## 6) Rules For Low-Confidence Rendering

- use restrained, non-dramatic styling for low-confidence recurrence
- avoid intensity, glow, or central placement that implies hidden importance
- low-confidence states should remain readable without theatrical emphasis

## 7) Rules Against Exaggerated Salience

- no dramatic visual amplification of weak signals
- no cinematic contrast that reframes weak support as major finding
- no symbolic emphasis that implies latent diagnosis or identity truth

## 8) Rules Against Hidden Suppression Bias

- suppression policy must be explicit and stable
- avoid silent suppression patterns that systematically erase inconvenient ambiguity
- suppression outcomes should be reviewable in QA evidence paths

## 9) Rules For Weak-Signal Inspectability

- weak signals must support trace-back to source evidence when displayed
- if suppressed, suppression reason should be explainable in verification artifacts
- weak-signal status should remain auditable for doctrine compliance

## 10) QA Weak-Signal Checks

PASS only if:
- weak signals remain bounded and visibly low-support
- suppression logic is consistent and reviewable
- low-support rendering avoids dramatic salience
- no hidden diagnosis/identity implication appears
- weak-signal evidence lineage remains inspectable

Automatic FAIL if:
- weak signals masquerade as strong recurrence
- low-support recurrence is stylized as atmospheric “latent truth”
- suppression hides signal without rationale path
- weak signals become decorative texture detached from evidence

## 11) Founder Decision Targets

- approve weak-signal bounded-rendering rule set
- approve threshold-visibility and suppression-rationale requirements
- approve QA fail triggers for exaggerated salience and hidden suppression bias

## Weak-Signal Guardrail

Weak signals should:
- remain bounded
- remain visibly low-support
- avoid dramatic styling
- avoid disappearing completely without rationale

Weak signals must not:
- imply hidden diagnosis
- masquerade as strong recurrence
- become atmospheric decoration
- create "latent truth" implication
