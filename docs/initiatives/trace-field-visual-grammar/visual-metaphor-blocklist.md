# Visual Metaphor Blocklist

Initiative: Trace Field Visual Grammar  
Continuation: Visual Metaphor Allowlist/Blocklist Decision Packet  
Scope: Docs-only doctrine and risk framing

## Purpose

Define visual metaphor families that are incompatible with Wayword doctrine because they imply hidden certainty, diagnosis, identity, or false scientific authority.

## 1) Personality Maps

Why it overclaims:
- implies stable latent identity typing from recurrence

Hidden implications:
- "this is who you are" framing

Likely user interpretation drift:
- recurrence read as trait diagnosis

Doctrine conflict:
- violates non-diagnostic and ambiguity-preserving posture

QA red flags:
- labels that look like personality classes or fixed profiles

## 2) Emotional Weather Systems

Why it overclaims:
- maps language recurrence to emotional states as if measured truth

Hidden implications:
- mood diagnosis and emotional scoring energy

Likely user interpretation drift:
- changing weather interpreted as mental-state certainty

Doctrine conflict:
- violates no emotional scoring and no therapy framing

QA red flags:
- storm/temperature/intensity visual semantics tied to emotional meaning

## Blocklist Rule

If a proposed visual metaphor family creates hidden diagnostic, identity, or certainty implications at first glance, it is blocked even if technical evidence links exist in the background.
