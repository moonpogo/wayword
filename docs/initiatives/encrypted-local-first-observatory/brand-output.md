# Brand/Marketing Output

Initiative: Encrypted Local-First Observatory Architecture  
Lane: Brand/Marketing  
Scope: internal positioning constraints only (no public campaign)

## Internal Positioning Constraints

If and when this initiative matures, Wayword may frame:
- ownership: writing remains user-owned and local-first
- privacy: sync continuity can exist without server-readable writing
- continuity: observatory depth can persist across devices without changing doctrine

## Boundaries For Future Messaging

Brand may not:
- overpromise cryptographic guarantees
- imply safety/therapy outcomes
- imply emotional certainty or hidden understanding
- frame privacy as absolute beyond verified architecture

## Approved Direction (Internal)

- trust through restraint and verifiable behavior
- continuity without surveillance posture
- privacy and doctrine continuity as co-equal values

## Prohibited Direction (Internal)

- fear-based security hype
- growth-hack privacy slogans
- convenience-first messaging that weakens ownership posture
- account/payments messaging that blurs observatory boundaries

## Brand Status

Brand planning PASS for internal-only constrainting.
Public messaging is NO-GO until founder decision and technical verification are complete.
