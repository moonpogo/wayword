Initiative: Encrypted Local-First Observatory Architecture  
Lane: Editorial Doctrine  
Scope: internal wording constraints for privacy/account/sync language

## Approved Internal Wording Direction

Use restrained, test-backed phrasing:
- "local-first by default"
- "encrypted before sync"
- "deletion includes derived observatory artifacts"
- "account continuity does not change observatory interpretation boundaries"
- "privacy guarantees are tied to verifiable system behavior"

## Prohibited Wording

Do not use:
- "military-grade encryption"
- "zero risk"
- "fully anonymous in all contexts"
- "we can always recover your data"
- "we never store anything" (unless literally true for all paths)
- vague trust language without architecture evidence

## Tone Constraints

- plain, restrained, non-hype
- no certainty inflation
- no therapy-adjacent language
- no implied hidden interpretation capability

## Claim Discipline Rules

- no privacy claim without architectural and QA backing
- no account/sync claim that implies server-readable writing
- no deletion claim that excludes derived artifacts
